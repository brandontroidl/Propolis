A volume candidate is an address that reached a thousand established TCP events. It is published only if you approve it here. Approve publishes this address in the retention feeds; it is not reported to abuse vendors. Reject keeps it out, and it is not offered again. The count is what the sensors' logs declared, so look at what the address did before approving it. The exception: on a node started with PROPOLIS_FEED_VOLUME_AUTO_PUBLISH=true every candidate is published whatever is decided here, and delisting an address is the way to take it out.
